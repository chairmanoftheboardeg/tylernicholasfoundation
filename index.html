Our mission

Empowering Young People. Creating Opportunities. Building Seychelles.

The Tyler Nicholas Foundation is a national youth platform. We find and share real opportunities, run our own programs, and give young Seychellois across every island the skills, confidence and doors they need — so the next generation can lead.
