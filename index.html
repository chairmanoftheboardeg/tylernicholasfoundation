What we do

Five pillars, one mission

Everything we do sits under five areas of work — led by our Directors General and built to help every young person learn, lead, connect, create and serve.

Youth Development & Education

Workshops and skills — public speaking, leadership, financial and digital literacy, and the TNF Youth Academy.

Opportunities & Partnerships

Finding, verifying and sharing scholarships, internships and exchanges — and building partnerships that open doors.

Community & Civic Engagement

District outreach, volunteering and civic education so young people understand and shape the society they live in.

Innovation & Entrepreneurship

Technology, digital access and enterprise — helping young people and local businesses build and grow.

National Programs & Exchanges

Flagship national programs that connect young Seychellois across islands, schools and the wider world.

Flagship programs

Experiences we create ourselves

We don't only share opportunities made by others — we build our own, so young Seychellois can do things that were out of reach before.

TNF Model United Nations

Debate, diplomacy and public speaking in a simulated UN — open to young people nationwide.

National Youth Island Exchange

Live with a host family on another island, attend a different school, and strengthen national unity.

School Exchange Program

Spend a week at another school and share ideas on how to make school life better for everyone.

TNF Youth Academy

Practical workshops in leadership, communication, finance, technology and career readiness.

TNF Volunteer Network

A growing network of youth volunteers serving communities across Seychelles.
