About the Foundation

A national youth platform working across Seychelles — for the youth, for the islands, for tomorrow.

Home / About

A national platform for young Seychellois

The Tyler Nicholas Foundation is a youth-focused organisation working across Seychelles to help young people access opportunities, build skills and serve their communities. We believe that meaningful change begins when people choose to serve, create opportunities, and take responsibility for the communities around them.

We do three things. We find and verify opportunities — scholarships, internships, exchanges and more — and bring them together in one place. We run our own programmes in leadership, education, entrepreneurship and community service. And we build partnerships between young people, families, businesses, institutions and government.

The Foundation is still at the beginning of its journey, but our ambitions are significant. We are building something transparent, responsible and focused on real, measurable impact — a platform through which many people can contribute their ideas, skills, time and resources toward something larger than themselves.

Our mission

To empower young Seychellois with the opportunities, skills and support they need to learn, lead, serve and shape a better future.

Our vision

A Seychelles where every young person — on every island — has a fair chance to grow, contribute and lead.
